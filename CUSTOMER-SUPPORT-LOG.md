
# Customer Support Log

## Project Overview

The Customer Support Log is a practical portfolio document designed to demonstrate customer service, administrative support, communication, and problem-solving skills. It records customer queries, actions taken, and resolution status.

## Customer Support Objectives

- Record customer queries accurately.
- Prioritize customer requests according to urgency.
- Track actions taken to resolve customer issues.
- Maintain clear and professional communication.
- Monitor unresolved queries and follow-ups.
- Improve customer satisfaction through timely support.

## Priority Definitions

- **High:** Urgent requests that require immediate attention.
- **Medium:** Important requests that should be handled promptly.
- **Low:** General requests that can be handled when time is available.

## Status Definitions

- **Not started:** The query has not yet been handled.
- **In progress:** The query is currently being investigated or resolved.
- **Resolved:** The customer query has been addressed.

## Customer Service Improvement

The customer support log helps the business monitor response times, identify recurring customer concerns, improve record-keeping, and provide consistent customer service.
